Security and responsible AI

For us, security isn’t a badge but a set of questions we ask on every project. This page sets out what we commit to as a company and what we decide with you on each project.

What we commit to

These principles apply to every project, whatever its size or technology.

  1. The data first, then the solution

    Before we propose a solution, we establish which data is involved and how sensitive it is. That helps decide which solutions are possible.

  2. As little access as necessary

    People, applications and AI agents get only the permissions they need for their task.

  3. Traceable data flows

    We document which data flows where and where it is processed — so your data protection officer can assess it.

  4. No training on your data

    Your data is not used to train third-party models unless you explicitly agree. Where we can’t ensure that, we advise against the service.

  5. People stay accountable

    Where AI-supported processes prepare consequential decisions, a person makes the final call.

Which operating model fits is decided with you on each project — based on the data, the requirements and your existing IT.

AI in practice

  • Evaluate before use

    We test answer quality against your own examples before an AI application goes live.

  • Monitor in operation

    Answers, errors and costs are logged and reviewed regularly.

  • A plan for mistakes

    Every AI application has a path for cases where the model is unsure or wrong — usually to a person.

  • Clear limits for agents

    AI agents act only within defined permissions; consequential steps are confirmed.

Data protection and your data protection officer

We support your data protection officer with documentation of data flows, information for the record of processing activities, and input for a data protection impact assessment where one is needed.

We establish which contracts with service providers are required, such as data processing agreements, and review the terms of the services used.

What we don’t promise

  • We don’t hand out blanket badges such as “GDPR-compliant”. Whether a processing activity is lawful depends on the specific project and is assessed there.
  • We are not a law firm. Where a legal assessment is needed, we say so and work with your legal advisers.
  • There is no such thing as absolute security. We tell you which risks remain and how we keep them small.

Data protection isn’t new to us: in 2018, our founder supported the GDPR implementation for the products he was responsible for at IONOS.

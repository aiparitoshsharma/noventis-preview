Enter data once, use it everywhere

When systems don’t talk to each other, people do the translating — retyping, spreadsheets and follow-up questions. We connect your systems so data is entered once and arrives where it’s needed.

When companies come to us

  • “We type the same data into three systems.”
  • “Every department has its own spreadsheet — and every one shows different numbers.”
  • “Our online shop and our ERP know nothing about each other.”
  • “Someone spends two days pulling together the monthly report.”
  • “We want to replace a system, but everything depends on it.”

What we do

  1. Connect systems

    We connect ERP, CRM, shop, accounting and specialist applications through interfaces — using existing standards where they exist and building connections where they don’t.

  2. Make processes end-to-end

    An order flows from intake through production to invoice without anyone copying data by hand. Checks sit where corrections are made today.

  3. Put master data in order

    Customers, products and suppliers exist only once — with clear ownership of who maintains what.

  4. Reporting you can trust

    Reports and dashboards take their numbers straight from the source systems, update themselves and show where every figure comes from.

  5. Replace systems step by step

    Where an old system has to go, we build the new one alongside it and move the work over piece by piece. Operations carry on.

How we measure success, for example

  • hours of manual entry per week
  • errors per month in transfers
  • time until the monthly report is ready

Security and data protection

More on security and responsible AI

  • Every interface gets only the permissions it needs for its job.
  • Data flows are documented so your data protection officer can assess them.
  • Personal data is transferred only to where it is needed.
  • Credentials live in a secured store, not in the code.

Technology decisions with reasons

Cloud or your own servers, standard software or a custom build, this vendor or that one: we assess the options independently, give our recommendation in writing with reasons, and stay with the implementation until it runs.

When companies come to us

  • “We’re facing a technology decision and want an independent view.”
  • “Three vendors, three quotes — and we can’t compare them.”
  • “We’re supposed to move to the cloud. But what exactly, and what stays here?”
  • “Our IT project has been running for months and nobody knows where it stands.”
  • “We need someone who translates between management and the technical side.”

What we do

  1. Assess options

    We compare solutions, vendors and quotes against criteria agreed with you up front — including the cost over the full term.

  2. Architecture that holds

    We design or review your systems’ architecture: what connects to what, where the risks are, and what will still fit in five years.

  3. Plan cloud and modernisation

    We work out what belongs in the cloud and what doesn’t, and plan modernisation in steps that each work on their own and can be reversed.

  4. Lead projects to a result

    We take on project leadership or stabilise a running project: clear milestones, honest status, decisions at the right time.

  5. Translate between business and technology

    We frame technical questions so management can decide — and business goals so the technical side can deliver them.

What you hold at the end, for example

  • a reasoned decision, in writing
  • a plan with steps, costs and risks
  • an honest project status everyone understands

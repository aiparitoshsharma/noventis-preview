AI and automation that pays off

Not every task needs AI. We find the ones where it genuinely pays off and implement them so that you know where your data is and who is accountable.

When companies come to us

  • “We should be doing something with AI — but where do we start?”
  • “Our people spend hours copying orders, invoices or delivery notes out of emails.”
  • “The knowledge is in manuals, tickets and old projects — but nobody can find it.”
  • “We have an AI prototype, but it never makes it into production.”
  • “We want to use AI, but our data must not leave the building.”

What we do

  1. Find and assess use cases

    We look at how you work and assess where AI or conventional automation saves time, prevents errors or raises quality — and where it doesn’t.

  2. Process documents and emails

    Incoming orders, invoices or forms are read, checked and transferred into your systems. Uncertain cases go to a person.

  3. Make internal knowledge searchable

    Employees ask questions in their own words and get answers from your own documents — with sources, and only with the access they already have.

  4. AI agents with clear limits

    Agents that complete tasks across several systems get exactly the permissions they need. What they do is logged; consequential steps are confirmed by a person.

  5. From prototype to production

    We turn experiments into dependable applications: with tests, evaluation of answer quality, monitoring in operation and a plan for when the model gets it wrong.

How we measure success, for example

  • handling time per case
  • share of cases correct without rework
  • time to find an answer in internal knowledge

For your IT team

We choose technology to fit your environment and your data, not our preferences. Typical choices:

Models
Large language models via the EU regions of the major cloud providers, contractually bounded API providers, or open models on your own infrastructure
Patterns
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agents with tool permissions, structured extraction, classification
Operation
Evaluation against test sets, logging, monitoring, fallbacks and human approval
Connection
Via APIs to ERP, CRM, DMS, email and specialist applications

Security and data protection

More on security and responsible AI

  • Before deployment, we establish which data the model sees and where it is processed.
  • With external AI services, we check contractually whether your data is stored or used for training.
  • Access rights apply to the AI too: it only sees what the person asking is allowed to see.
  • A person stays accountable for consequential decisions.

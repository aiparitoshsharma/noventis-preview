About Noventis Innovations

We help companies exactly where business and technology meet — founder-led, based in Pforzheim, with a network of experienced specialists.

Why Noventis exists

Good technology rarely fails because of the technology. It fails at the translation: between what a business needs and what eventually gets built. In between lie misunderstandings, solutions in search of a problem, and projects nobody is steering any more.

Noventis Innovations exists to close that gap. We start with the problem, choose the technology accordingly, and stay accountable until the solution works in operation — and demonstrably helps.

Paritosh Sharma

Founder and your point of contact

Paritosh Sharma is an electrical engineer who began his career in industrial automation: four years of drives, controls and connecting plant equipment to ERP systems in a steel tube plant in India.

After his MBA at Hochschule Pforzheim, he moved into strategy and product management — via Deloitte’s innovation unit The Garage to IONOS, where as product manager for cloud storage and SaaS products he held P&L responsibility and, in 2018, supported the GDPR implementation for his products.

At Bosch Power Tools, he has been responsible for connected products and digital offerings since 2019 and today leads a team for software and digital innovation in after-sales — including AI applications.

This mix of engineering, business understanding and delivery experience is the core of Noventis: understand what a business needs, choose the right technology, and make sure it works day to day. He has lived and worked in Germany for more than ten years and works in German and English.

Our network

For implementation, we work with an established network of experienced specialists in software development, integration, data, cloud and AI. For each project we assemble the team it needs — no more, no less. Accountability for the result stays with us.

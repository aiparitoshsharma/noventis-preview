How we work together

You always know where your project stands, what happens next and what you decide. And you won’t be pushed into a solution that was decided before we met.

Four steps, each with a decision

  1. First conversation

    You describe the situation in your own words; we ask questions. 30 minutes, in person or by video, free of charge.

    You get
    An honest view of whether and how we can help — even if the answer is that you don’t need us.
    You decide
    Whether to take the next step.

  2. Analysis

    We look at your processes, systems and data and agree with you the key figure we will measure success by. Scope and price are agreed up front — a short free exploration or a paid analysis, depending on the size of the question.

    You get
    A written plan: what we would change, in which steps, at what price, and how each step can be reversed.
    You decide
    Whether, when and with whom to implement. The plan is yours.

  3. Implementation

    We implement step by step, scheduled around your operations. The existing process stays in place until the new one has proved itself.

    You get
    A regular, honest status: what’s done, what’s open, what has changed.
    You decide
    After each step, whether to continue.

  4. Measure and hand over

    We measure the agreed key figure again and report the result in writing. Then we look after the solution or hand it over, documented, to your team.

    You get
    The result in black and white, documentation, source code and credentials.
    You decide
    Whether we continue to support, extend or close.

One key figure, agreed in advance

We don’t promise savings — we measure them. Before we start, we agree one figure that tells us whether it worked. We measure it in your company, not from an industry statistic, and report the result even when it isn’t what we hoped. What doesn’t help gets switched off; unlike a machine, software can be taken back.

How we keep risk small

  • Small, reversible steps

    We change one part at a time. Every step can be undone, and the existing process stays available until the new one has proved itself in daily use.

  • What we build belongs to you

    Source code, credentials, configuration and documentation are yours. We use widely adopted, openly documented technology so another provider could take over.

  • One accountable contact

    You deal with the founder, not with changing project teams. He stays accountable for the result, even when specialists are involved.

  • Clear agreements before go-live

    How quickly we respond to a fault and who operates what is written into the contract before anything goes into your operations.

Who does the work

The founder leads the first conversation, the analysis and the accountability for the result. For implementation, we assemble the team your project needs from an established network of experienced specialists — in development, integration, data, cloud or AI. Everyone works to the same standards, and you always have the same point of contact.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to know what solution we want?

No. It’s enough to know what isn’t working. Finding the right solution is part of our job — and sometimes it’s simpler than you expect.

Are we too small for this — or too large?

We work with companies of very different sizes. Scope, budget and pace follow you. In large organisations we often work for one department or a clearly defined project.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing. It takes about 30 minutes, in person or by video. Afterwards you know whether and how we can help.

Who owns the result?

You do: source code, credentials, documentation and reports. You can also implement the plan from the analysis with someone else.

What if it doesn’t work?

Then the agreed key figure shows it, and we tell you. Because we work in small steps and the existing process stays in place, a step can be reversed without your operations suffering.

Do you work on site?

In the region around Pforzheim, happily on site; elsewhere in Germany mostly remotely — with on-site meetings where they help.

Tell us where things are stuck.

You don’t need to know the solution. Describe your situation in a few sentences. We’ll reply within one working day and arrange a free 30-minute first conversation.

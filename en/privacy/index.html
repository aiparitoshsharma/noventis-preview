Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process your name, email address, message and — if you provide them — company and phone number in order to answer your enquiry and arrange a first conversation. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Your details are transmitted encrypted to a function at our hosting provider and sent from there to us as an email. For sending we use: Open:confirm contact form email delivery (planned: Azure Communication Services, data location Europe). Your details are not stored on the website itself.

To protect against automated submissions, the form contains a field invisible to people and measures the time until it is sent. No cookies are set and no further data is collected for this.

We delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Contact by email

If you email us, we process your details on the same legal basis and delete them under the same conditions as with the contact form.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21). An informal message to the address above is sufficient.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
